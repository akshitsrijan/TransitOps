# Emergency Response Manual

## 1. Introduction

This Emergency Response Manual provides comprehensive guidelines and procedures for all employees of TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. to effectively respond to various emergency situations. The primary objectives are to protect human life, minimize injuries, safeguard company assets, mitigate environmental impact, and ensure business continuity. Adherence to these protocols is mandatory for all personnel.

## 2. General Emergency Procedures

### 2.1. Assess the Situation

Upon encountering an emergency, immediately assess the nature and severity of the situation. Identify any immediate threats to life or property.

### 2.2. Ensure Personal Safety

Prioritize your own safety and the safety of others. Do not attempt to handle a situation that puts you at undue risk.

### 2.3. Activate Alarm/Notify Authorities

If necessary, activate alarms (e.g., fire alarm) and immediately notify the appropriate emergency services (Police, Fire, Ambulance) by dialing the emergency numbers listed in Section 16.

### 2.4. Notify TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch

Immediately contact the TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch (24/7 hotline: [Insert 24/7 Number Here]) to report the incident. Provide clear and concise information:
*   Your name and contact number.
*   Exact location of the emergency.
*   Nature of the emergency (e.g., vehicle accident, fire, medical emergency).
*   Number of people involved and apparent injuries.
*   Any hazardous materials involved.

### 2.5. Follow Instructions

Comply with instructions from emergency services personnel and TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch.

## 3. Vehicle Accident

### 3.1. Immediate Actions at the Scene

1.  **Stop Safely:** Pull the vehicle over to a safe location, if possible, away from traffic. Turn on hazard lights.
2.  **Secure the Scene:** Place warning triangles or flares to alert oncoming traffic.
3.  **Check for Injuries:** Assess yourself and any passengers for injuries. Provide first aid if trained and safe to do so.
4.  **Call Emergency Services:** If there are injuries, significant damage, or if the vehicle is obstructing traffic, call Police (100) and Ambulance (102) immediately.
5.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch immediately.

### 3.2. Information Gathering

Collect the following information:
*   Date, time, and exact location of the accident.
*   Names, contact numbers, and insurance details of all parties involved.
*   Vehicle details (make, model, license plate, VIN) of all vehicles involved.
*   Names and contact numbers of any witnesses.
*   Police report number (if applicable).
*   Take clear photographs of the accident scene, vehicle damage, and road conditions from multiple angles.

### 3.3. Do Not Admit Fault

Do not admit fault or discuss liability at the scene. Provide only factual information to police and insurance representatives.

## 4. Driver Injury

### 4.1. Minor Injury

For minor injuries, administer first aid using the vehicle’s first-aid kit. Report the injury to TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch and complete an incident report.

### 4.2. Serious Injury

1.  **Call Ambulance:** Immediately call an ambulance (102).
2.  **Provide First Aid:** If trained, provide immediate first aid without moving the injured person unless absolutely necessary for their safety.
3.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch immediately, providing all details.
4.  **Secure Scene:** If the injury occurred at a company site, secure the area to prevent further injury and preserve evidence for investigation.

## 5. Cargo Theft

### 5.1. Immediate Actions

1.  **Ensure Safety:** If you witness a theft in progress, do not intervene if it puts you at risk. Prioritize personal safety.
2.  **Call Police:** Immediately report the theft to the Police (100).
3.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch immediately, providing details of the cargo, vehicle, location, and any suspects.

### 5.2. Post-Incident

*   **Do Not Touch:** Do not touch anything at the scene that could compromise evidence.
*   **Cooperate with Police:** Provide all requested information to law enforcement.
*   **Incident Report:** Complete a detailed incident report, including a list of stolen items, estimated value, and any relevant circumstances.

## 6. Vehicle Theft

### 6.1. Immediate Actions

1.  **Call Police:** Immediately report the vehicle theft to the Police (100).
2.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch immediately, providing the vehicle registration number, make, model, last known location, and any tracking device information.

### 6.2. Post-Incident

*   **Cooperate with Police:** Provide all requested information to law enforcement.
*   **Insurance Claim:** Assist the company in filing an insurance claim.

## 7. Hijacking

### 7.1. Driver Protocol

In the extremely rare and dangerous event of a hijacking:

*   **Comply:** Do not resist. Your life is paramount. Comply with the hijackers’ demands.
*   **Observe:** Pay close attention to details about the hijackers (appearance, voice, weapons, vehicle used) without making it obvious.
*   **Activate Covert Alarm:** If the vehicle is equipped with a covert alarm or tracking device, activate it discreetly if safe to do so.
*   **Do Not Engage:** Do not attempt to negotiate or engage in any heroic actions.

### 7.2. Post-Incident

1.  **Report Immediately:** As soon as you are safe, report the incident to the Police (100) and TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch.
2.  **Provide Details:** Provide all observed details to the authorities and the company for investigation.
3.  **Seek Support:** Psychological support will be provided to drivers who experience such traumatic events.

## 8. Fire

### 8.1. Small Fire (Vehicle or Premises)

Refer to the Safety Manual, Section 3.3 for Vehicle Fire Procedure. For small fires on premises, use appropriate fire extinguishers if trained and safe to do so. Evacuate if the fire cannot be controlled immediately.

### 8.2. Large Fire (Vehicle or Premises)

1.  **Evacuate:** Immediately evacuate all personnel from the affected area/vehicle.
2.  **Activate Alarm:** Activate the nearest fire alarm (if on premises).
3.  **Call Fire Services:** Call the Fire Department (101) immediately.
4.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch.
5.  **Assembly Point:** Proceed to the designated emergency assembly point.

## 9. Flood

### 9.1. Driver Protocol

Refer to the Safety Manual, Section 6.2 for Flood Procedure. Do not drive through flooded areas. If caught in a flood, abandon the vehicle and move to higher ground if safe.

### 9.2. Premises Flood

1.  **Cut Power:** If safe, cut off electrical power to the affected area.
2.  **Secure Assets:** Move valuable assets to higher ground.
3.  **Notify Management:** Inform facility management and TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch.
4.  **Do Not Enter:** Do not enter flooded areas if the water level is high or if there is a risk of electrical shock.

## 10. Landslide

### 10.1. Driver Protocol

Refer to the Safety Manual, Section 6.3 for Landslide Procedure. Observe warning signs and move away from the path of a landslide. Report to authorities and dispatch.

### 10.2. Premises Landslide

If company premises are located in a landslide-prone area and a landslide occurs or is imminent:

1.  **Evacuate:** Immediately evacuate all personnel to a safe, designated assembly point.
2.  **Notify Authorities:** Call emergency services.
3.  **Notify Management:** Inform facility management and TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch.

## 11. Engine Failure

### 11.1. Driver Protocol

1.  **Stop Safely:** Pull over to the side of the road as safely as possible. Turn on hazard lights.
2.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch, providing location and details of the failure.
3.  **Await Assistance:** Remain with the vehicle in a safe location until assistance arrives.

## 12. Brake Failure

### 12.1. Driver Protocol

In the event of brake failure while driving:

1.  **Stay Calm:** Remain calm and do not panic.
2.  **Pump Brakes:** Rapidly pump the brake pedal to try and build up hydraulic pressure.
3.  **Downshift:** Shift to a lower gear to use engine braking.
4.  **Parking Brake:** Gradually apply the parking brake (handbrake) while holding the release button to avoid locking the wheels.
5.  **Steer to Safety:** Steer the vehicle to a safe area, such as a shoulder or escape ramp.
6.  **Notify Dispatch:** Once safe, inform TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch.

## 13. Tyre Burst

### 13.1. Driver Protocol

In the event of a tyre burst:

1.  **Grip Steering Wheel Firmly:** Hold the steering wheel tightly to maintain control.
2.  **Do Not Brake Suddenly:** Avoid sudden braking. Gradually ease off the accelerator.
3.  **Steer Straight:** Try to keep the vehicle moving straight.
4.  **Slow Down Gradually:** Allow the vehicle to slow down on its own, then gently apply brakes.
5.  **Pull Over Safely:** Once the vehicle has slowed sufficiently, steer to a safe location off the road.
6.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch.

## 14. Medical Emergency (Non-Driver Specific)

### 14.1. On Premises

1.  **Assess:** Determine if the person is conscious and breathing.
2.  **Call Ambulance:** If serious, call Ambulance (102).
3.  **First Aid:** Administer first aid if trained.
4.  **Notify Supervisor:** Inform the immediate supervisor and TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch.
