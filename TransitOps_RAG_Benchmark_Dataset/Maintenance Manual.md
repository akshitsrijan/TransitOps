# Maintenance Manual

## 1. Introduction

This Maintenance Manual outlines the procedures and guidelines for the maintenance and repair of all vehicles operated by TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. The objective is to ensure the fleet remains in optimal operational condition, maximizing safety, reliability, and longevity while minimizing downtime and operational costs. All maintenance personnel and drivers are required to adhere to the policies and procedures detailed herein.

## 2. Types of Maintenance

### 2.1. Preventive Maintenance (PM)

Preventive Maintenance (PM) is a proactive approach to vehicle care, involving scheduled inspections, servicing, and replacement of parts to prevent breakdowns and extend vehicle life. PM is critical for ensuring continuous operation and compliance with safety regulations.

### 2.2. Corrective Maintenance (CM)

Corrective Maintenance (CM) is performed when a vehicle component fails or is found to be defective during inspections. Its purpose is to restore the vehicle to its operational state as quickly and efficiently as possible.

## 3. Key Maintenance Procedures

### 3.1. Oil Changes

Engine oil and filter changes are performed at regular intervals as specified by the vehicle manufacturer or every 10,000 km, whichever comes first. This ensures proper engine lubrication and prevents premature wear.

### 3.2. Brake Inspection and Service

Brake systems are inspected every 20,000 km or as part of every second oil change. This includes checking brake pads, discs, drums, fluid levels, and hydraulic lines. Worn components are replaced immediately to ensure optimal braking performance.

### 3.3. Tyre Replacement and Rotation

Tyres are inspected daily during pre-trip checks for pressure, tread depth, and damage. Rotation is performed every 20,000 km to ensure even wear. Tyres are replaced when tread depth falls below the legal limit or when significant damage is observed.

### 3.4. Battery Maintenance

Batteries are inspected during routine service for corrosion, terminal tightness, and charge level. Water levels in lead-acid batteries are checked and topped up as necessary. Batteries are replaced every 2-3 years or when they fail to hold a charge.

### 3.5. Air Filter Replacement

Engine air filters are replaced every 40,000 km or more frequently in dusty environments, as indicated by inspection. Clean air filters are essential for engine performance and fuel efficiency.

### 3.6. Coolant Replacement

Engine coolant is checked during every service and replaced every 80,000 km or every 2 years, whichever comes first. This prevents engine overheating and corrosion within the cooling system.

### 3.7. Engine Diagnostics

Advanced diagnostic tools are used to identify and troubleshoot engine-related issues. Regular diagnostic checks are performed as part of PM, and immediately when a fault indicator light illuminates.

### 3.8. Transmission Service

Transmission fluid and filter are changed according to manufacturer specifications, typically every 100,000 km. This ensures smooth gear changes and extends the life of the transmission.

### 3.9. Suspension System Inspection

Suspension components, including shocks, springs, and bushings, are inspected during every major service for wear and damage. Proper suspension is crucial for vehicle stability and driver comfort.

### 3.10. Electrical System Checks

The entire electrical system, including wiring, lights, and charging system, is inspected during routine maintenance. Faulty components are repaired or replaced to ensure all electrical functions are operational.

### 3.11. Air Conditioning System Maintenance

AC systems are checked annually for refrigerant levels, leaks, and proper functioning. Filters are replaced as needed to ensure clean and efficient cooling.

### 3.12. Emission System Maintenance

Emission control systems are inspected and maintained to ensure compliance with environmental regulations. This includes checking the Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF), Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) system, and Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) system.

### 3.13. Fuel System Maintenance

Fuel filters are replaced at regular intervals, typically every 40,000 km, to prevent contaminants from reaching the engine. Fuel lines and tanks are inspected for leaks and damage.

## 4. Lubrication Schedule

All critical moving parts, including chassis components, steering linkages, and universal joints, are lubricated according to the vehicle manufacturer’s recommendations, typically during every service interval.

## 5. Maintenance Intervals

Maintenance intervals are determined based on vehicle type, mileage, operating conditions, and manufacturer recommendations. A detailed schedule is maintained in the Fleet Management System (FMS) for each vehicle.

## 6. Parts Replacement Policy

Only genuine or approved OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) equivalent parts are to be used for all repairs and replacements. This ensures compatibility, reliability, and maintains vehicle warranty.

## 7. Workshop Procedures

### 7.1. Safety First

All workshop activities must adhere to strict safety protocols, including the use of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), proper handling of tools and chemicals, and maintaining a clean and organized workspace.

### 7.2. Repair Workflow

1.  **Vehicle Reception:** Vehicle is brought into the workshop, and initial assessment is performed.
2.  **Diagnosis:** Technicians use diagnostic tools and visual inspection to identify the root cause of the issue.
3.  **Repair Authorization:** For non-routine repairs, authorization is obtained from the Fleet Manager.
4.  **Repair Execution:** Repairs are performed by qualified technicians following established procedures.
5.  **Quality Control:** After repairs, a quality control check and road test are performed to ensure the issue is resolved.
6.  **Vehicle Handover:** Vehicle is returned to service with updated maintenance records.

## 8. Inspection Checklist

A comprehensive inspection checklist is utilized for all preventive maintenance services. This checklist ensures that all critical components are thoroughly examined and serviced. The checklist is updated periodically to reflect new vehicle models and industry best practices.
