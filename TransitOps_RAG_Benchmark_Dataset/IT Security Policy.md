# IT Security Policy

## 1. Introduction

This IT Security Policy outlines the principles, rules, and guidelines for the secure use of information technology resources at TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. The objective is to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the company's information assets, systems, and data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction. All employees, contractors, and third-party users are required to adhere to this policy.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all information systems, networks, applications, data, and devices owned or managed by TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd., as well as all personnel who access or use these resources, regardless of their location or the device used.

## 3. Password Policy

### 3.1. Objective

To ensure the use of strong, unique, and regularly changed passwords to protect user accounts and systems from unauthorized access.

### 3.2. Requirements

*   **Complexity:** Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
*   **Uniqueness:** Passwords must not be reused across different systems or accounts.
*   **Change Frequency:** Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
*   **Prohibited Passwords:** Passwords must not contain personal information (e.g., name, birth date), common words, or sequences (e.g., 

'123456', 'password').
*   **Storage:** Passwords must never be written down or shared with anyone. Use an approved password manager if necessary.

## 4. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

### 4.1. Objective

To ensure that users are granted access only to the information and systems necessary to perform their job functions, adhering to the principle of least privilege.

### 4.2. Implementation

*   **Access Matrix:** An access matrix defines the roles within the company and the specific permissions associated with each role.
*   **Regular Review:** User access rights are reviewed quarterly by department heads and IT to ensure they remain appropriate and are revoked upon job change or termination.
*   **Segregation of Duties:** Critical functions are segregated to prevent a single individual from having complete control over a process.

## 5. Authentication

### 5.1. Objective

To verify the identity of users attempting to access company resources.

### 5.2. Methods

*   **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** MFA is mandatory for accessing all critical systems and remote access. This typically involves a password combined with a second factor (e.g., a code from a mobile app, a physical token).
*   **Single Sign-On (SSO):** Where applicable, SSO is implemented to streamline access while maintaining security.

## 6. Data Privacy

### 6.1. Objective

To protect sensitive company and customer data from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction, in compliance with relevant data protection regulations.

### 6.2. Principles

*   **Data Classification:** All data is classified based on its sensitivity (e.g., Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted).
*   **Encryption:** Sensitive data, both in transit and at rest, must be encrypted using approved cryptographic standards.
*   **Data Minimization:** Only necessary data should be collected and retained for the required period.
*   **Access Control:** Access to sensitive data is strictly controlled and logged.
*   **Employee Training:** All employees receive regular training on data privacy best practices and their responsibilities.

## 7. Audit Logs

### 7.1. Objective

To maintain detailed records of system activities to facilitate security monitoring, incident investigation, and compliance auditing.

### 7.2. Requirements

*   **Logging:** All critical system events, including logins, access attempts, data modifications, and administrative actions, are logged.
*   **Retention:** Audit logs are retained for a minimum of one year and are securely stored to prevent tampering.
*   **Monitoring:** Logs are regularly reviewed for suspicious activities or security breaches.

## 8. User Permissions

### 8.1. Objective

To manage and control the specific actions users can perform within systems and applications.

### 8.2. Management

*   **Least Privilege:** Users are granted the minimum permissions required to perform their job functions.
*   **Regular Audits:** User permissions are audited regularly to ensure they align with current job responsibilities.
*   **Automated Provisioning/Deprovisioning:** User accounts and permissions are provisioned and deprovisioned automatically upon hiring, job change, or termination to ensure timely access control.

## 9. Network Security

### 9.1. Firewall Management

All network perimeters are protected by firewalls configured to block unauthorized access and traffic. Firewall rules are regularly reviewed and updated.

### 9.2. Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems (IDPS)

IDPS are deployed to monitor network traffic for malicious activities and prevent intrusions.

### 9.3. VPN for Remote Access

All remote access to the company network must be conducted via an approved Virtual Private Network (VPN) connection.

## 10. Endpoint Security

### 10.1. Anti-Malware Protection

All company-owned devices (laptops, desktops, servers) must have approved anti-malware software installed and kept up-to-date.

### 10.2. Patch Management

Operating systems and applications on all devices are regularly patched and updated to address known vulnerabilities.

### 10.3. Device Encryption

All company-owned laptops and mobile devices that store sensitive data must have full disk encryption enabled.

## 11. Incident Response

### 11.1. Objective

To establish a structured approach for handling security incidents, from detection to recovery, to minimize impact and restore normal operations.

### 11.2. Procedure

1.  **Detection:** Identify security incidents through monitoring systems, user reports, or audits.
2.  **Reporting:** All security incidents must be reported immediately to the IT Security Team.
3.  **Containment:** Take immediate steps to contain the incident and prevent further damage.
4.  **Eradication:** Remove the cause of the incident (e.g., malware, unauthorized access).
5.  **Recovery:** Restore affected systems and data from backups.
6.  **Post-Incident Review:** Conduct a review to identify root causes and implement preventive measures.

## 12. Employee Responsibilities

All employees are responsible for:
*   Adhering to all aspects of this IT Security Policy.
*   Protecting their login credentials and not sharing them.
*   Reporting any suspected security incidents or vulnerabilities immediately.
*   Completing mandatory security awareness training.
