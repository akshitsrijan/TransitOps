# Fuel Management Manual

## 1. Introduction

This Fuel Management Manual outlines the policies and procedures for the efficient and secure management of fuel resources at TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. Effective fuel management is crucial for controlling operational costs, minimizing environmental impact, and preventing theft or misuse. All employees involved in fuel handling, purchasing, and reconciliation are required to adhere to these guidelines.

## 2. Fuel Filling Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

### 2.1. Objective

To ensure a consistent, safe, and accurate process for refueling company vehicles.

### 2.2. Procedure

1.  **Vehicle Positioning:** Position the vehicle safely at the designated fueling station, ensuring the engine is turned off and the parking brake is engaged.
2.  **Driver Identification:** The driver must present their company ID and fuel card to the station attendant or use the automated system.
3.  **Fuel Type Verification:** Confirm the correct fuel type (e.g., Diesel, Petrol, Electric Charge) for the vehicle before fueling commences.
4.  **Fueling:** Dispense the required amount of fuel. Do not overfill the tank.
5.  **Data Entry:** Accurately record the following information:
    *   Date and time of fueling.
    *   Vehicle registration number.
    *   Odometer reading.
    *   Quantity of fuel dispensed (liters).
    *   Cost per liter and total cost.
    *   Fuel station name and location.
6.  **Receipt Collection:** Obtain a printed receipt for every fuel transaction.
7.  **Post-Fueling Check:** Ensure the fuel cap is securely replaced and there are no leaks.
8.  **Reporting:** Submit all fuel receipts and recorded data to the designated department at the end of each shift or trip.

## 3. Fuel Theft Prevention

### 3.1. Objective

To implement measures that deter and detect fuel theft, protecting company assets.

### 3.2. Prevention Strategies

*   **Secure Fuel Storage:** Company-owned fuel tanks must be secured with locks and monitored by surveillance systems.
*   **Fuel Card Control:** Implement strict controls over fuel card issuance, usage limits, and PIN management. Cards should be assigned to specific drivers or vehicles.
*   **GPS Tracking:** Utilize GPS tracking systems to monitor vehicle routes and detect unauthorized stops or deviations that could indicate siphoning.
*   **Regular Audits:** Conduct unannounced audits of fuel consumption records against vehicle mileage and trip logs.
*   **Driver Training:** Educate drivers on the importance of fuel security and the consequences of theft.
*   **Tamper-Evident Seals:** Use tamper-evident seals on fuel tanks for vehicles parked overnight or for extended periods.

## 4. Fuel Card Policy

### 4.1. Objective

To establish clear guidelines for the issuance, use, and management of company fuel cards.

### 4.2. Policy Details

*   **Issuance:** Fuel cards are issued to authorized drivers only upon completion of necessary training and agreement to the terms of use.
*   **Personal Use Prohibited:** Fuel cards are strictly for company vehicle use only. Personal use is prohibited and will result in disciplinary action, including termination.
*   **PIN Confidentiality:** Drivers are responsible for keeping their PIN confidential and must not share it with anyone.
*   **Lost or Stolen Cards:** Any lost or stolen fuel card must be reported immediately to the Fuel Manager and the card issuer for deactivation.
*   **Transaction Limits:** Daily or weekly transaction limits may be imposed on fuel cards to control expenditure.
*   **Receipt Retention:** Drivers must retain all fuel receipts and submit them as per the Fuel Filling SOP.

## 5. Fuel Reconciliation

### 5.1. Objective

To accurately match fuel purchases with vehicle consumption and identify discrepancies.

### 5.2. Reconciliation Process

1.  **Data Collection:** Gather all fuel transaction data from fuel card statements and driver-submitted receipts.
2.  **Mileage Data:** Obtain odometer readings and mileage data from the FMS or driver logs.
3.  **Comparison:** Compare fuel dispensed (from receipts/card data) with fuel consumed (based on mileage and vehicle-specific fuel efficiency rates).
4.  **Discrepancy Investigation:** Investigate any significant discrepancies. This may involve reviewing GPS data, driver logs, and interviewing personnel.
5.  **Reporting:** Generate regular fuel reconciliation reports for management review, highlighting consumption trends and any identified issues.

## 6. Mileage Calculation

### 6.1. Objective

To accurately calculate vehicle mileage for fuel efficiency analysis, maintenance scheduling, and operational cost tracking.

### 6.2. Calculation Method

Mileage is primarily calculated using GPS tracking data from the FMS. In cases where GPS data is unavailable or for verification, odometer readings recorded during pre-trip and post-trip inspections are used.

## 7. Fuel Efficiency Monitoring

### 7.1. Objective

To continuously monitor and improve the fuel efficiency of the fleet, reducing costs and environmental impact.

### 7.2. Monitoring and Improvement Strategies

*   **KPI Tracking:** Track Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as kilometers per liter (KPL) for each vehicle and driver.
*   **Driver Training:** Provide regular training on fuel-efficient driving techniques (e.g., smooth acceleration, anticipating traffic, proper gear selection).
*   **Vehicle Maintenance:** Ensure vehicles are regularly maintained according to the Maintenance Manual to keep engines running efficiently.
*   **Route Optimization:** Utilize route planning software to minimize travel distances and avoid congested areas.
*   **Idle Reduction:** Enforce the idle vehicle policy to reduce unnecessary fuel consumption.
*   **Technology Adoption:** Explore and adopt new technologies that enhance fuel efficiency, such as aerodynamic aids or alternative fuel vehicles.

## 8. Fuel Fraud Detection

### 8.1. Objective

To identify and investigate instances of fuel fraud, protecting the company from financial losses.

### 8.2. Detection Methods

*   **Anomaly Detection:** Use FMS data analytics to identify unusual fueling patterns, such as excessive fuel purchases for a given mileage, fueling at unusual locations, or multiple transactions in a short period.
*   **Receipt Verification:** Cross-reference physical receipts with electronic fuel card data.
*   **Surveillance:** Utilize CCTV at company depots and conduct random checks at external fueling stations.
*   **Whistleblower Policy:** Encourage employees to report suspicious activities through a confidential whistleblower program.
*   **Data Analysis:** Regular analysis of fuel consumption reports to spot trends that may indicate fraudulent activity.
