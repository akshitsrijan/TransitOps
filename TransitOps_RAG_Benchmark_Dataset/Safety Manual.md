# Safety Manual

## 1. Introduction

This Safety Manual outlines the comprehensive safety policies, procedures, and guidelines for all employees and operations of TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. Our commitment to safety is paramount, aiming to provide a safe working environment, prevent accidents, protect company assets, and ensure compliance with all applicable safety regulations. All personnel are required to understand and adhere to the principles and practices detailed in this manual.

## 2. Road Safety

### 2.1. General Principles

Road safety is a shared responsibility. Drivers are expected to operate vehicles defensively, courteously, and in strict adherence to all traffic laws. Non-driving personnel are also responsible for promoting a culture of safety on and off the road.

### 2.2. Safe Driving Practices

*   **Adherence to Traffic Laws:** Strictly follow all speed limits, traffic signals, and road signs.
*   **Defensive Driving:** Anticipate hazards, maintain safe following distances, and be aware of surroundings.
*   **Vehicle Condition:** Ensure the vehicle is in optimal working condition through pre-trip inspections.
*   **Distraction-Free Driving:** Avoid using mobile phones or engaging in any distracting activities while driving.
*   **Fatigue Management:** Comply with driving hours regulations and take mandatory rest breaks.

## 3. Fire Safety

### 3.1. Prevention

*   **No Smoking Policy:** Smoking is strictly prohibited in all company vehicles, premises, and near fuel storage areas.
*   **Flammable Materials:** Store flammable materials in designated, well-ventilated areas away from ignition sources.
*   **Electrical Safety:** Ensure all electrical equipment is in good working order and not overloaded.
*   **Regular Inspections:** Conduct regular inspections of vehicles and facilities for potential fire hazards.

### 3.2. Fire Extinguishers

All company vehicles and premises are equipped with appropriate fire extinguishers. Employees must be trained in their proper use. Extinguishers must be regularly inspected and maintained.

### 3.3. Vehicle Fire Procedure

In the event of a vehicle fire:

1.  **Stop Safely:** Pull over to a safe location away from traffic and flammable materials.
2.  **Turn Off Engine:** Switch off the engine and remove the keys.
3.  **Evacuate:** Ensure all occupants evacuate the vehicle immediately and move to a safe distance.
4.  **Call Emergency Services:** Dial emergency services (e.g., 101 in India) and provide exact location and details.
5.  **Attempt Extinguishment (if safe):** If the fire is small and it is safe to do so, use a fire extinguisher. Do not put yourself at risk.
6.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics dispatch immediately.

## 4. Medical Emergency

### 4.1. First Aid

All company vehicles are equipped with a first-aid kit. Key personnel, including drivers, receive basic first-aid training. In case of minor injuries, administer first aid if trained.

### 4.2. Emergency Response

In a serious medical emergency:

1.  **Call Emergency Services:** Immediately call emergency medical services (e.g., 102 in India) and provide clear details of the situation and location.
2.  **Provide Assistance (if safe):** If trained and it is safe to do so, provide immediate assistance to the injured person.
3.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics dispatch immediately, providing all available details.

## 5. Hazardous Material Spill

### 5.1. Containment and Reporting

In the event of a hazardous material spill:

1.  **Ensure Safety:** Prioritize personal safety. If the spill poses an immediate threat, evacuate the area.
2.  **Contain (if safe):** If trained and equipped, attempt to contain the spill to prevent its spread.
3.  **Call Emergency Services:** Immediately contact emergency services (e.g., fire department, HAZMAT team) and provide details of the substance and quantity.
4.  **Notify Dispatch:** Inform TransitOps Logistics dispatch immediately, who will then notify relevant authorities and the client.

## 6. Natural Disasters

### 6.1. Earthquake Procedure

*   **During an Earthquake:** If driving, pull over to a clear area away from buildings, bridges, and overhead wires. Stay inside the vehicle. If indoors, drop, cover, and hold on.
*   **After an Earthquake:** Check for injuries. Assess vehicle/property damage. Follow instructions from emergency services. Do not drive if roads are damaged.

### 6.2. Flood Procedure

*   **Avoid Flooded Areas:** Never attempt to drive through flooded roads. Turn around, don't drown.
*   **Evacuate (if necessary):** If caught in a flood, abandon the vehicle and move to higher ground if safe to do so.
*   **Notify Dispatch:** Report flooded routes or areas to dispatch.

### 6.3. Landslide Procedure

*   **Observe Warning Signs:** Be aware of warning signs like cracks in roads, leaning trees, or unusual sounds.
*   **Evacuate:** If a landslide is imminent or occurring, move away from its path to a safe area.
*   **Notify Authorities:** Report landslides or potential landslide areas to local authorities and dispatch.

## 7. Vehicle-Specific Emergencies

### 7.1. Fuel Leakage

*   **Stop Safely:** Pull over immediately to a safe, well-ventilated area.
*   **Turn Off Engine:** Switch off the engine.
*   **No Ignition Sources:** Ensure no open flames or sparks are nearby.
*   **Notify Dispatch:** Report the leakage to dispatch, who will arrange for immediate assistance.

### 7.2. Electrical Fire

*   **Turn Off Engine:** If safe, switch off the engine and disconnect the battery.
*   **Use Appropriate Extinguisher:** Use a Class C (electrical) fire extinguisher. Do not use water.
*   **Evacuate and Call:** If the fire spreads, evacuate and call emergency services.

### 7.3. Chemical Exposure

*   **Identify Chemical:** If possible, identify the chemical involved.
*   **First Aid:** Follow first aid guidelines for chemical exposure (e.g., flush skin/eyes with water).
*   **Seek Medical Attention:** Seek immediate medical attention and provide information about the chemical.
*   **Notify Dispatch:** Report the incident to dispatch.

## 8. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements

### 8.1. General PPE

All employees, especially drivers and maintenance personnel, are required to use appropriate PPE as per their job roles. This includes:

*   **Safety Vests:** High-visibility vests must be worn when working near traffic or in low-light conditions.
*   **Safety Footwear:** Steel-toed safety shoes are mandatory in workshops and during cargo handling.
*   **Gloves:** Appropriate gloves must be worn for handling cargo, performing maintenance, or refueling.

### 8.2. Specialized PPE

For specific tasks, such as handling hazardous materials or performing certain maintenance procedures, specialized PPE (e.g., respirators, chemical-resistant suits, eye protection) will be provided and its use is mandatory.

## 10. Incident Reporting

### 10.1. Immediate Reporting

All accidents, injuries, property damage, hazardous spills, or any other safety incidents, regardless of severity, must be reported immediately to the TransitOps Logistics Emergency Dispatch.

### 10.2. Detailed Reporting

A detailed incident report must be submitted within 24 hours of the occurrence. This report should include:

*   Date, time, and location of the incident.
*   Description of the incident.
*   Persons involved and witnesses.
*   Injuries sustained or property damaged.
*   Actions taken.
*   Photographs, if applicable.

## 11. Near Miss Reporting

### 11.1. Definition

A 
near miss is an unplanned event that did not result in injury, illness, or damage – but had the potential to do so. Reporting near misses is crucial for preventing future accidents.

### 11.2. Reporting Procedure

All employees are encouraged to report near misses through the designated reporting system. This information is used for analysis and to implement corrective actions, not for disciplinary purposes.

## 12. Safety Audits

### 12.1. Purpose

Regular safety audits are conducted to assess the effectiveness of safety policies and procedures, identify potential hazards, and ensure compliance with internal and external regulations. Audits help in continuous improvement of our safety management system.

### 12.2. Audit Schedule and Scope

Safety audits are conducted quarterly by the Safety Officer and external auditors annually. The scope includes vehicle inspections, driver practices, workshop safety, and compliance with all safety manuals.
