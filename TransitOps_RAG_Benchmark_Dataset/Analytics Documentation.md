# Analytics Documentation

## 1. Introduction

This Analytics Documentation outlines the key performance indicators (KPIs), methodologies, and reporting structures used by TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. to monitor, analyze, and improve its operational efficiency, financial performance, and safety standards. Effective data analytics is crucial for informed decision-making, identifying trends, optimizing resource allocation, and achieving strategic objectives. This document serves as a guide for all stakeholders involved in data collection, analysis, and interpretation.

## 2. Fleet Utilization

### 2.1. Objective

To measure how effectively the company's fleet of vehicles is being used, aiming to maximize operational hours and minimize idle time.

### 2.2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*   **Vehicle Utilization Rate:**
    $$\text{Utilization Rate} = \frac{\text{Total Operational Hours}}{\text{Total Available Hours}} \times 100\%$$
    *   **Operational Hours:** Time a vehicle is actively engaged in a trip (driving, loading, unloading).
    *   **Available Hours:** Total hours a vehicle is available for use (e.g., 24 hours/day, 7 days/week, excluding scheduled maintenance).
*   **Average Daily Kilometers (ADK):** Total kilometers driven by a vehicle over a period divided by the number of operational days.
*   **Idle Time Percentage:**
    $$\text{Idle Time Percentage} = \frac{\text{Total Idle Hours}}{\text{Total Operational Hours}} \times 100\%$$
    *   **Idle Hours:** Time a vehicle's engine is running but the vehicle is stationary.

### 2.3. Data Sources

*   Fleet Management System (FMS) telematics data (GPS tracking, engine hours).
*   Trip logs and dispatch records.

### 2.4. Analysis and Reporting

Monthly reports highlight utilization rates by vehicle type, branch, and driver. Low utilization rates trigger investigations into scheduling inefficiencies, maintenance delays, or underutilized assets.

## 3. Fuel Efficiency

### 3.1. Objective

To monitor and improve the fuel consumption performance of the fleet, thereby reducing operational costs and environmental impact.

### 3.2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*   **Kilometers Per Liter (KPL):**
    $$\text{KPL} = \frac{\text{Total Kilometers Driven}}{\text{Total Fuel Consumed (Liters)}}$$
    *   Tracked for individual vehicles, vehicle types, and drivers.
*   **Fuel Cost Per Kilometer:** Total fuel cost divided by total kilometers driven.
*   **Fuel Consumption Variance:** Actual fuel consumption versus planned/budgeted consumption.

### 3.3. Data Sources

*   FMS telematics data.
*   Fuel card transaction data.
*   Driver fuel receipts.

### 3.4. Analysis and Reporting

Weekly and monthly reports compare KPL across the fleet, identify outliers (high consumption), and track the impact of fuel-efficient driving training and maintenance programs. Variances are investigated for potential issues like fuel theft or mechanical problems.

## 4. Operational Cost

### 4.1. Objective

To track and analyze all costs associated with fleet operations to identify areas for cost reduction and efficiency improvement.

### 4.2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*   **Cost Per Kilometer (CPK):**
    $$\text{CPK} = \frac{\text{Total Operational Costs}}{\text{Total Kilometers Driven}}$$
    *   Includes fuel, maintenance, driver wages, tolls, permits, and depreciation.
*   **Maintenance Cost Per Kilometer:** Total maintenance costs divided by total kilometers driven.
*   **Driver Wage Cost Per Kilometer:** Total driver wages divided by total kilometers driven.

### 4.3. Data Sources

*   Finance system (expense reports, payroll).
*   FMS (maintenance records, trip data).

### 4.4. Analysis and Reporting

Monthly reports provide a detailed breakdown of operational costs, allowing management to benchmark costs, identify cost drivers, and implement cost-saving initiatives. Trends in CPK are closely monitored.

## 5. Return on Investment (ROI)

### 5.1. Objective

To evaluate the financial benefits derived from investments in fleet assets, technology, or operational improvements.

### 5.2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*   **ROI for New Vehicles:** Calculated as per the Finance Manual (Section 5.2).
*   **ROI for Technology Upgrades:** Measures the financial return from investments in new FMS, route optimization software, or safety systems.

### 5.3. Data Sources

*   Finance system (capital expenditure, revenue data).
*   Operational data (efficiency gains, cost reductions).

### 5.4. Analysis and Reporting

ROI is calculated for major investments and reported to senior management to justify expenditures and assess the effectiveness of strategic decisions.

## 6. Trip KPIs

### 6.1. Objective

To measure the efficiency and effectiveness of individual trips and overall trip management.

### 6.2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*   **On-Time Delivery (OTD) Rate:**
    $$\text{OTD Rate} = \frac{\text{Number of On-Time Deliveries}}{\text{Total Number of Deliveries}} \times 100\%$$
*   **Average Transit Time:** The average time taken from pick-up to delivery.
*   **Load Factor:** The percentage of vehicle capacity utilized for cargo.
*   **Number of Incidents Per Trip:** Count of accidents, breakdowns, or delays reported per trip.

### 6.3. Data Sources

*   FMS (trip planning, real-time tracking, delivery confirmations).
*   Customer feedback.

### 6.4. Analysis and Reporting

Daily and weekly reports track trip performance, highlighting routes or drivers with consistent delays or high incident rates. This data informs route optimization, driver training, and customer service improvements.

## 7. Maintenance KPIs

### 7.1. Objective

To assess the effectiveness of the maintenance program in keeping the fleet operational and minimizing downtime.

### 7.2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*   **Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF):** Average time a vehicle operates before experiencing a breakdown.
*   **Mean Time To Repair (MTTR):** Average time taken to repair a vehicle after a breakdown.
*   **Preventive Maintenance (PM) Compliance Rate:** Percentage of scheduled PMs completed on time.
*   **Unscheduled Maintenance Percentage:** Percentage of maintenance events that are unscheduled breakdowns.

### 7.3. Data Sources

*   FMS maintenance module.
*   Workshop records.

### 7.4. Analysis and Reporting

Monthly reports analyze maintenance trends, identify recurring issues, and evaluate the performance of the maintenance team. High MTTR or low PM compliance indicates areas for process improvement.

## 8. Safety KPIs

### 8.1. Objective

To monitor and improve the safety record of the fleet and personnel, reducing accidents and incidents.

### 8.2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*   **Accident Rate:** Number of accidents per million kilometers driven.
*   **Incident Rate:** Number of reported incidents (including near misses) per million kilometers driven.
*   **Safety Violation Rate:** Number of traffic violations or internal safety policy breaches per driver.
*   **Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR):** Number of lost time injuries per million hours worked.

### 8.3. Data Sources

*   Incident reporting system.
*   Police reports.
*   FMS telematics data (hard braking, harsh acceleration, speeding alerts).

### 8.4. Analysis and Reporting

Monthly safety reports highlight trends in accidents and incidents, identify high-risk drivers or routes, and measure the effectiveness of safety training programs. This data is crucial for proactive safety management.

## 9. Driver Score

### 9.1. Objective

To evaluate individual driver performance across various metrics, promoting safe and efficient driving habits.

### 9.2. Scoring Components

Driver scores are typically a composite of:
*   **Safety Score:** Based on speeding incidents, harsh braking, harsh acceleration, and accident history.
*   **Efficiency Score:** Based on KPL, idle time, and adherence to planned routes.
*   **Compliance Score:** Based on adherence to driving hours regulations and documentation submission.
*   **Customer Feedback:** Ratings from delivery points.

### 9.3. Data Sources

*   FMS telematics data.
*   Trip logs.
*   Incident reports.
*   HR records.

### 9.4. Analysis and Reporting

Individual driver scorecards are generated monthly, providing feedback for performance reviews, training needs, and incentive programs. Low scores trigger targeted coaching and disciplinary actions if necessary.

## 10. Vehicle Score

### 10.1. Objective

To evaluate the overall performance and reliability of individual vehicles, informing maintenance schedules and replacement decisions.

### 10.2. Scoring Components

Vehicle scores are typically a composite of:
*   **Maintenance Cost:** Lower costs contribute to a higher score.
*   **Breakdown Frequency:** Fewer breakdowns lead to a higher score.
*   **Fuel Efficiency:** Higher KPL contributes to a higher score.
*   **Utilization Rate:** Higher utilization contributes to a higher score.
*   **Age and Mileage:** Newer vehicles with lower mileage may have a higher base score.

### 10.3. Data Sources

*   FMS maintenance module.
*   Fuel consumption reports.
*   Utilization reports.

### 10.4. Analysis and Reporting

Vehicle scorecards are generated quarterly, providing insights into vehicle reliability and cost-effectiveness. This data supports decisions on vehicle retirement, replacement, and identifying models with better performance.
