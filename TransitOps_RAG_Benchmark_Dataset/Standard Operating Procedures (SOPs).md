# Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for key processes within TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. These SOPs are designed to ensure consistency, efficiency, and compliance across all operations, minimizing errors and maximizing productivity. All employees are required to adhere to these procedures.

## 2. Trip Creation

### 2.1. Objective

To establish a standardized process for creating new trips in the Fleet Management System (FMS) based on client requests.

### 2.2. Procedure

1.  **Receive Transport Request:** Sales or client service team receives a transport request from a client, detailing cargo, origin, destination, delivery timeline, and any special requirements.
2.  **Verify Request Details:** The operations coordinator verifies the completeness and accuracy of the request, cross-referencing with client agreements if necessary.
3.  **Access FMS:** Log in to the Fleet Management System (FMS).
4.  **Initiate New Trip:** Navigate to the 
“New Trip” or “Create Shipment” module.
5.  **Enter Trip Details:** Input all relevant information:
    *   Client Name and Contact
    *   Cargo Description (type, weight, dimensions, special handling)
    *   Origin and Destination Addresses
    *   Requested Pick-up and Delivery Dates/Times
    *   Any specific instructions or notes.
6.  **Attach Supporting Documents:** Upload any relevant documents (e.g., purchase orders, special permits).
7.  **Save Draft Trip:** Save the trip as a draft for approval.
8.  **Notify Approver:** Send an automated notification or manual alert to the designated Trip Approver.

## 3. Trip Approval

### 3.1. Objective

To ensure all trips are reviewed and approved by authorized personnel before vehicle and driver assignment.

### 3.2. Procedure

1.  **Receive Approval Request:** The Trip Approver receives a notification for a new trip awaiting approval.
2.  **Review Trip Details:** Access the FMS and review all details of the proposed trip, including cargo, route, schedule, and associated costs.
3.  **Check Resources:** Verify the availability of suitable vehicles and qualified drivers for the proposed trip.
4.  **Assess Feasibility:** Evaluate the trip’s feasibility against operational capacity, regulatory compliance, and profitability.
5.  **Approve/Reject:** Based on the review:
    *   **Approve:** If all criteria are met, approve the trip. The FMS will then make the trip available for vehicle and driver assignment.
    *   **Reject:** If issues are identified, reject the trip, providing clear reasons and requesting necessary revisions from the trip creator.
6.  **Notify Creator:** Send an automated notification to the trip creator regarding the approval or rejection status.

## 4. Vehicle Assignment

### 4.1. Objective

To assign the most appropriate vehicle to an approved trip, optimizing utilization and ensuring compliance.

### 4.2. Procedure

1.  **Access Approved Trips:** The Dispatch Manager or authorized personnel accesses the list of approved trips in the FMS.
2.  **Identify Vehicle Requirements:** Review the trip details to determine the specific vehicle type, capacity, and features required (e.g., refrigerated, oversized).
3.  **Check Vehicle Availability:** Consult the FMS for available vehicles that match the requirements, considering:
    *   Current location and proximity to origin.
    *   Maintenance schedule and last service date.
    *   Vehicle type and capacity.
    *   Any special certifications or equipment.
4.  **Assign Vehicle:** Select the most suitable vehicle and assign it to the trip in the FMS.
5.  **Update Status:** Update the vehicle status to “Assigned” or “In-Transit Prep.”
6.  **Notify Driver:** If the driver is already assigned, notify them of the vehicle assignment.

## 5. Driver Assignment

### 5.1. Objective

To assign a qualified and compliant driver to an approved trip, ensuring adherence to driving hours regulations and safety standards.

### 5.2. Procedure

1.  **Access Approved Trips:** The Dispatch Manager or authorized personnel accesses the list of approved trips in the FMS.
2.  **Identify Driver Requirements:** Review the trip details for any specific driver qualifications (e.g., hazardous goods certification, interstate license).
3.  **Check Driver Availability:** Consult the FMS for available drivers, considering:
    *   Current location and proximity to origin.
    *   Driving hours availability (Hours of Service compliance).
    *   Required certifications and licenses.
    *   Rest period compliance.
4.  **Assign Driver:** Select the most suitable driver and assign them to the trip in the FMS.
5.  **Update Status:** Update the driver status to “Assigned” or “Pre-Trip Briefing.”
6.  **Notify Driver:** Send trip details and instructions to the assigned driver.

## 6. Maintenance Approval

### 6.1. Objective

To establish a formal process for approving vehicle maintenance requests, ensuring cost-effectiveness and operational continuity.

### 6.2. Procedure

1.  **Maintenance Request Submission:** A driver or maintenance technician identifies a vehicle defect or scheduled maintenance requirement and submits a maintenance request via the FMS or a designated form.
2.  **Initial Assessment:** The maintenance supervisor performs an initial assessment of the request, prioritizing based on urgency and impact on operations.
3.  **Cost Estimation:** For non-routine or significant repairs, the maintenance team obtains cost estimates from internal workshops or external service providers.
4.  **Approval Review:** The Operations Manager or Fleet Manager reviews the maintenance request and cost estimates.
5.  **Approve/Reject:**
    *   **Approve:** If approved, the maintenance work is scheduled, and necessary parts are ordered.
    *   **Reject:** If rejected, reasons are provided, and alternative solutions (e.g., vehicle retirement) may be considered.
6.  **Notify Originator:** The originator of the request is notified of the approval or rejection.

## 7. Fuel Approval

### 7.1. Objective

To control and monitor fuel consumption by establishing a clear approval process for bulk fuel purchases or significant fuel top-ups outside standard procedures.

### 7.2. Procedure

1.  **Fuel Request Submission:** A driver or operations personnel submits a request for fuel, specifying quantity, vehicle, and reason (e.g., long-haul trip, emergency top-up).
2.  **Review Request:** The Fuel Manager or designated approver reviews the request against trip plans, historical consumption, and budget.
3.  **Approve/Reject:**
    *   **Approve:** If approved, a fuel voucher or authorization code is issued.
    *   **Reject:** If rejected, reasons are provided.
4.  **Record Keeping:** All fuel approvals and consumption data are logged in the FMS for reconciliation and audit purposes.

## 8. Expense Approval

### 8.1. Objective

To ensure all work-related expenses incurred by drivers and operational staff are properly documented, reviewed, and approved.

### 8.2. Procedure

1.  **Expense Submission:** Employees submit expense reports with supporting receipts for items such as tolls, minor repairs, meals (within policy limits), and accommodation.
2.  **Manager Review:** The immediate supervisor reviews the expense report for adherence to company policy and accuracy.
3.  **Finance Approval:** The Finance Department conducts a final review and approves the expense for reimbursement.
4.  **Reimbursement:** Approved expenses are processed for payment according to the payroll schedule.

## 9. Incident Reporting

### 9.1. Objective

To establish a consistent and timely process for reporting all incidents, including accidents, breakdowns, cargo damage, and security breaches.

### 9.2. Procedure

1.  **Immediate Notification:** The first person aware of an incident (typically the driver) immediately notifies the Emergency Dispatch Hotline.
2.  **Initial Report:** The dispatcher records initial details: date, time, location, type of incident, and persons involved.
3.  **Detailed Report Submission:** Within 24 hours, a detailed incident report is submitted via the FMS or a dedicated incident reporting system, including:
    *   Full description of the incident.
    *   Witness statements.
    *   Photographs/videos.
    *   Damage assessment.
    *   Actions taken.
4.  **Investigation:** The Safety or Operations Department initiates an investigation into the incident.
5.  **Follow-up Actions:** Corrective and preventive actions are identified and implemented.

## 10. Vehicle Retirement

### 10.1. Objective

To establish a systematic process for retiring vehicles from the fleet due to age, mileage, irreparable damage, or economic inefficiency.

### 10.2. Procedure

1.  **Identification for Retirement:** Vehicles are identified for retirement based on maintenance records, operational costs, and fleet replacement schedules.
2.  **Evaluation:** The Fleet Manager and Finance Department evaluate the vehicle’s residual value and potential for sale or salvage.
3.  **Approval:** The decision to retire a vehicle is approved by senior management.
4.  **Decommissioning:** The vehicle is removed from active service, all company branding is removed, and necessary documentation (e.g., registration cancellation) is processed.
5.  **Disposal:** The vehicle is sold, auctioned, or salvaged according to company policy and environmental regulations.

## 11. Vehicle Purchase

### 11.1. Objective

To establish a structured process for acquiring new vehicles to expand or replace the existing fleet.

### 11.2. Procedure

1.  **Needs Assessment:** The Operations and Fleet Management departments identify the need for new vehicles based on operational demand, fleet expansion plans, or replacement schedules.
2.  **Specification Development:** Detailed specifications for the required vehicles (type, capacity, features, emissions standards) are developed.
3.  **Vendor Selection:** Potential vendors are identified, and bids are solicited. A thorough evaluation of proposals, including cost, quality, and after-sales service, is conducted.
4.  **Approval:** The purchase decision is approved by senior management and the finance department.
5.  **Procurement:** Purchase orders are issued, and the acquisition process is managed.
6.  **Integration:** New vehicles are registered, insured, equipped with necessary tracking and safety devices, and integrated into the FMS.

## 12. Driver Suspension

### 12.1. Objective

To outline the process for suspending a driver due to policy violations, safety incidents, or regulatory non-compliance.

### 12.2. Procedure

1.  **Incident/Violation Identification:** A policy violation or incident requiring potential suspension is identified (e.g., traffic violation, accident, drug policy violation).
2.  **Investigation:** HR and Operations conduct a thorough investigation, gathering all facts and evidence.
3.  **Review and Recommendation:** The findings are reviewed, and a recommendation for suspension (or other disciplinary action) is made.
4.  **Approval:** The suspension is approved by HR management and, if necessary, senior operations management.
5.  **Notification:** The driver is formally notified of the suspension, including reasons, duration, and any conditions for reinstatement.
6.  **FMS Update:** The driver’s status in the FMS is updated to “Suspended,” preventing them from being assigned to trips.

## 13. License Renewal

### 13.1. Objective

To ensure all drivers maintain valid driving licenses and necessary endorsements, preventing operational disruptions due to expired credentials.

### 13.2. Procedure

1.  **Monitoring:** HR maintains a database of all driver licenses and their expiry dates. Automated alerts are generated several months before expiry.
2.  **Driver Notification:** Drivers are notified well in advance of their license renewal date.
3.  **Renewal Process:** Drivers are responsible for initiating and completing their license renewal process.
4.  **Verification:** Upon renewal, drivers must submit proof of their updated license to HR.
5.  **FMS Update:** HR updates the driver’s license information in the FMS.
6.  **Follow-up:** HR follows up with drivers who have not submitted proof of renewal by the expiry date, and if necessary, the driver is temporarily removed from active duty until valid documentation is provided.
