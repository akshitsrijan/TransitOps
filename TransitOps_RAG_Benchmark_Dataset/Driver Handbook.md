# Driver Handbook

## 1. Introduction

This Driver Handbook provides essential information and guidelines for all drivers employed by TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. It outlines the company's expectations regarding safe driving practices, operational procedures, and professional conduct. Adherence to these guidelines is mandatory to ensure the safety of our drivers, cargo, and the public, as well as to maintain the company's reputation and compliance with all relevant regulations.

## 2. Daily Checklist

Before commencing any trip, drivers must complete a comprehensive daily checklist to ensure the vehicle is in optimal condition for operation. This checklist covers critical safety and operational components.

### 2.1. Pre-Trip Inspection Items

*   **Tires:** Check pressure, tread depth, and for any visible damage.
*   **Brakes:** Test brake function, including parking brake.
*   **Lights:** Verify all headlights, tail lights, brake lights, turn signals, and hazard lights are operational.
*   **Fluids:** Check engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, and windshield washer fluid levels.
*   **Wipers:** Ensure windshield wipers are functional and blades are in good condition.
*   **Mirrors:** Adjust all mirrors for optimal visibility.
*   **Horn:** Test horn functionality.
*   **Safety Equipment:** Confirm presence and functionality of fire extinguisher, first-aid kit, warning triangles, and reflective vests.
*   **Documentation:** Ensure all required vehicle and cargo documents are present and valid.

## 3. Vehicle Inspection

### 3.1. Pre-Trip Inspection (PTI)

Drivers are required to perform a thorough Pre-Trip Inspection (PTI) before each journey. This inspection is crucial for identifying potential mechanical issues that could compromise safety or lead to breakdowns. Any defects found must be reported immediately to the maintenance department and rectified before departure.

### 3.2. Post-Trip Inspection (PoTI)

Upon completion of a trip, a Post-Trip Inspection (PoTI) must be conducted. This helps identify any damage or issues that may have occurred during the journey, allowing for timely repairs and ensuring the vehicle is ready for its next assignment.

## 4. Safe Driving Practices

### 4.1. General Principles

Safety is the paramount concern at TransitOps Logistics. Drivers are expected to operate vehicles responsibly, defensively, and in strict accordance with all traffic laws and company policies.

### 4.2. Defensive Driving

Defensive driving involves anticipating potential hazards and taking proactive measures to prevent accidents. Key principles include:

*   **Maintain Safe Following Distance:** Allow ample space between your vehicle and the vehicle ahead.
*   **Scan Ahead:** Continuously scan the road for potential dangers, including other vehicles, pedestrians, and road conditions.
*   **Be Aware of Surroundings:** Check mirrors frequently and be aware of blind spots.
*   **Anticipate Actions of Others:** Assume other drivers may make mistakes and be prepared to react.

### 4.3. Speed Limits

Drivers must strictly adhere to all posted speed limits and adjust speed according to road, weather, and traffic conditions. Exceeding speed limits is a serious violation and will result in disciplinary action.

### 4.4. Seatbelt Policy

All drivers and passengers must wear seatbelts at all times while the vehicle is in motion. This is a non-negotiable safety requirement.

## 5. Rest Periods and Fatigue Management

### 5.1. Driving Hours Regulations

Drivers must strictly comply with all government regulations regarding driving hours and mandatory rest periods. These regulations are in place to prevent driver fatigue and ensure road safety.

### 5.2. Fatigue Management

*   **Recognize Fatigue Signs:** Drivers must be aware of the signs of fatigue (e.g., yawning, heavy eyelids, difficulty concentrating) and take immediate action.
*   **Mandatory Breaks:** Utilize scheduled rest breaks to recuperate.
*   **Report Fatigue:** If a driver feels too fatigued to drive safely, they must immediately inform dispatch.

## 6. Mobile Phone Usage

Using a mobile phone for calls, texting, or any other purpose while driving is strictly prohibited. Drivers must pull over to a safe location if they need to use their phone.

## 7. Accident Reporting

### 7.1. Immediate Actions

In the event of an accident, drivers must:

1.  **Ensure Safety:** Secure the scene, activate hazard lights, and place warning triangles.
2.  **Check for Injuries:** Assess for injuries to themselves, passengers, and others involved.
3.  **Notify Authorities:** Contact local law enforcement and emergency services if necessary.
4.  **Notify Dispatch:** Immediately report the accident to TransitOps Logistics dispatch.

### 7.2. Information Gathering

Drivers must collect relevant information, including:

*   Date, time, and location of the accident.
*   Names, contact information, and insurance details of all parties involved.
*   Vehicle details (make, model, license plate) of all vehicles involved.
*   Witness contact information.
*   Photographs of the accident scene, vehicle damage, and road conditions.

## 8. Breakdown Reporting

In case of a vehicle breakdown, drivers must follow the procedure outlined in the Fleet Operations Manual (Section 16.1).

## 9. Customer Interaction

Drivers are often the face of TransitOps Logistics. Professionalism, courtesy, and effective communication are expected at all times when interacting with clients, consignees, and the public.

## 10. Fuel Filling Procedure

### 10.1. Authorized Fuel Stations

Drivers must refuel at authorized fuel stations as designated by TransitOps Logistics. Use of unauthorized stations is prohibited.

### 10.2. Fuel Card Usage

Company-issued fuel cards must be used for all fuel purchases. Drivers are responsible for safeguarding their fuel cards and reporting any loss or theft immediately.

### 10.3. Record Keeping

Accurate records of fuel purchases, including date, time, quantity, and odometer reading, must be maintained and submitted as per company policy.

## 11. Trip Completion

Upon completion of a trip, drivers must:

1.  **Verify Delivery:** Ensure all cargo has been successfully delivered and obtain necessary proof of delivery (POD).
2.  **Post-Trip Inspection:** Conduct a Post-Trip Inspection (PoTI).
3.  **Submit Documentation:** Submit all trip-related documents, including waybills, PODs, and fuel receipts, to the appropriate department.

## 12. Vehicle Cleaning

Drivers are responsible for maintaining the cleanliness of their assigned vehicles, both interior and exterior. Regular cleaning contributes to a professional image and extends vehicle lifespan.

## 13. Parking

Vehicles must be parked in designated, safe, and secure locations. Drivers must adhere to all parking regulations and avoid parking in areas that could obstruct traffic or pose a safety hazard.

## 14. Security

Drivers are responsible for the security of their vehicles and cargo. This includes:

*   **Locking Vehicles:** Always lock the vehicle when unattended.
*   **Securing Cargo:** Ensure cargo is properly secured and protected from theft.
*   **Reporting Suspicious Activity:** Report any suspicious activity or security concerns to dispatch immediately.

## 15. Driving in Specific Conditions

### 15.1. Night Driving

Refer to the Fleet Operations Manual (Section 6) for detailed guidelines on night driving safety protocols.

### 15.2. Rain Driving

*   **Reduce Speed:** Significantly reduce speed in wet conditions.
*   **Increase Following Distance:** Allow extra space for braking.
*   **Use Headlights:** Turn on headlights to improve visibility.
*   **Avoid Hydroplaning:** Be aware of standing water and avoid sudden maneuvers.

### 15.3. Hill Driving

*   **Gear Selection:** Use lower gears when ascending and descending steep grades to maintain control and prevent brake fade.
*   **Braking:** Use engine braking and intermittent braking to control speed on descents.

### 15.4. Highway Driving

*   **Maintain Lane Discipline:** Stay in the appropriate lane and avoid frequent lane changes.
*   **Awareness:** Be extra vigilant for high-speed traffic and large vehicles.
*   **Rest:** Take regular breaks to combat fatigue on long highway journeys.
