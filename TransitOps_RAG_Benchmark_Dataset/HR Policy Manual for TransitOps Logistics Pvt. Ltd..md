# HR Policy Manual for TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd.

## 1. Introduction

This HR Policy Manual outlines the policies and procedures governing human resources management at TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. It serves as a guide for employees and management, ensuring fair and consistent treatment, compliance with labor laws, and the promotion of a productive and harmonious work environment. These policies are designed to support our company's vision, mission, and values, fostering a culture of respect, integrity, and excellence.

## 2. Recruitment and Selection

### 2.1. Equal Employment Opportunity
TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. is committed to equal employment opportunities for all individuals. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national origin, age, disability, marital status, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law. All employment decisions are based on merit, qualifications, and business needs.

### 2.2. Job Descriptions
All positions within the company will have a clearly defined job description outlining responsibilities, qualifications, reporting structure, and performance expectations. These descriptions will be reviewed and updated periodically.

### 2.3. Recruitment Process
The recruitment process involves:
*   **Job Requisition:** Department heads submit a requisition for new or vacant positions.
*   **Advertising:** Positions are advertised internally and/or externally through appropriate channels.
*   **Application Review:** Applications are screened against job requirements.
*   **Interviews:** Qualified candidates undergo a structured interview process.
*   **Assessments:** Skills tests or other assessments may be conducted as required.
*   **Background Checks:** Reference checks, educational verification, and criminal background checks are conducted for final candidates.
*   **Offer of Employment:** Successful candidates receive a formal offer letter detailing terms and conditions of employment.

### 2.4. Onboarding
New hires will participate in an onboarding program to facilitate their integration into the company. This includes orientation, introduction to colleagues, review of policies, and job-specific training.

## 3. Compensation and Benefits

### 3.1. Salary Administration
Compensation is determined based on job responsibilities, market rates, individual qualifications, and performance. Salary reviews are conducted annually, considering performance appraisals and company financial health.

### 3.2. Statutory Benefits
TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. complies with all statutory requirements regarding employee benefits, including:
*   **Provident Fund (PF):** Contributions are made by both employer and employee as per the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 [3].
*   **Employee State Insurance (ESI):** Applicable employees receive benefits under the Employee State Insurance Act, 1948.
*   **Gratuity:** Gratuity is paid to eligible employees as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 [4].
*   **Maternity Benefit:** Female employees are entitled to maternity leave and benefits as per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 [5].

### 3.3. Other Benefits
Additional benefits may include health insurance, life insurance, accidental insurance, and other welfare schemes, subject to company policy and eligibility criteria.

### 3.4. Deductions
All statutory and authorized deductions (e.g., income tax, loan repayments) will be made from employee salaries as per applicable laws and employee consent.

## 4. Working Hours and Leave

### 4.1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are [e.g., 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday], with a [e.g., one-hour] lunch break. Specific departmental schedules may vary and will be communicated by respective managers. Overtime work must be pre-approved.

### 4.2. Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to [e.g., 15] days of paid annual leave per year, accruing monthly. Leave applications must be submitted in advance and approved by the supervisor. Unused leave may be carried over or encashed as per company policy.

### 4.3. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to [e.g., 7] days of paid sick leave per year. For absences exceeding [e.g., 2] days, a medical certificate is required. Employees must inform their supervisor as soon as possible.

### 4.4. Public Holidays
A list of declared public holidays will be circulated at the beginning of each calendar year. Employees are entitled to paid leave on these days.

### 4.5. Maternity/Paternity Leave
As per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 [5], eligible female employees are entitled to maternity leave. Paternity leave is also provided as per company policy. Details are available from HR.

### 4.6. Other Leaves
Special leaves such as bereavement leave, leave for jury duty, or leave without pay may be granted at the discretion of management, based on individual circumstances and company policy.

## 5. Performance Management

### 5.1. Performance Appraisals
Performance appraisals are conducted annually to evaluate employee performance, set goals, identify training needs, and discuss career development. This process involves self-assessment, manager review, and a feedback discussion.

### 5.2. Goal Setting
Employees will work with their supervisors to set clear, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) goals that align with departmental and company objectives.

### 5.3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
Employees whose performance consistently falls below expectations may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). The PIP outlines specific areas for improvement, required actions, and a timeline for review.

## 6. Employee Conduct and Discipline

### 6.1. Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to adhere to the company's Code of Conduct, which emphasizes honesty, integrity, respect, and professionalism. Violations may lead to disciplinary action.

### 6.2. Workplace Harassment and Discrimination
TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. maintains a strict policy against harassment and discrimination. Any form of harassment, including sexual harassment, bullying, or discrimination based on protected characteristics, is prohibited. Employees are encouraged to report incidents to HR or management [6].

### 6.3. Disciplinary Procedure
Disciplinary actions will be taken for violations of company policies, misconduct, or unsatisfactory performance. The procedure typically involves:
*   Verbal Warning
*   Written Warning
*   Suspension
*   Termination

The severity of the action will depend on the nature and frequency of the offense.

### 6.4. Grievance Procedure
Employees who have a grievance related to their employment should follow the established grievance procedure, starting with their immediate supervisor and escalating to HR if necessary. All grievances will be handled confidentially and fairly.

## 7. Training and Development

TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. is committed to investing in the continuous learning and development of its employees. We provide various training programs, workshops, and opportunities for skill enhancement to support career growth and ensure employees have the necessary competencies.

## 8. Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE)

### 8.1. Workplace Safety
Safety is paramount at TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. All employees are responsible for adhering to safety policies, reporting hazards, and participating in safety training. The company provides a safe working environment in compliance with the Factories Act, 1948, and other relevant regulations [7].

### 8.2. Health and Well-being
The company promotes employee health and well-being through various initiatives, including access to medical facilities, wellness programs, and support for work-life balance.

### 8.3. Environmental Responsibility
We are committed to environmentally responsible practices in all our operations, minimizing our ecological footprint and complying with environmental regulations.

## 9. Employee Separation

### 9.1. Resignation
Employees wishing to resign must provide written notice to their supervisor and HR department, typically [e.g., 30 or 60 days] in advance, as per their employment contract.

### 9.2. Termination
Employment may be terminated by the company due to performance issues, policy violations, redundancy, or other legitimate business reasons, in accordance with applicable labor laws.

### 9.3. Exit Formalities
Upon separation, employees are required to complete all exit formalities, including returning company property, settling dues, and participating in an exit interview.

## 10. Policy Review and Amendments

This HR Policy Manual will be reviewed periodically and may be amended or updated at the discretion of TransitOps Logistics Pvt. Ltd. Employees will be informed of any significant changes. The latest version will always be available through the HR department.
